What is different, and why. The page is one desk, not a row of cards: statement left, compiler right, hairline between. The editor is the single dark instrument — gcc’s diagnostic voice (file:line:col, severity in its own words, plain-language fix) is borrowed for the live linter instead of inventing a new error style.
